Manchester United: a team portrait, 2014–2026

Twelve Premier League seasons and ten managers, from Louis van Gaal to Michael Carrick, in underlying numbers. It uses match-level xG, pressing and deep completions for every league game, full event data (every pass, press and touch) for 2015–16, and season-level event counts for the two most recent seasons.

  • The press drained away over seven years. United had the league's most intense press in 2014–15 (Understat PPDA 7.1, 1st of 20). It weakened season by season to 12.8 (12th) in 2021–22 and has sat around 10–12 since.
  • Van Gaal's side had the ball, not the chances. In 2015–16 United had the league's highest share of passes and field tilt but ranked 20th of 20 for the speed of its attacks and 13th for xG.
  • 2023–24 was the defensive low point. Ten Hag's second season conceded 1.83 npxG per game, 15th in the league and the worst of the twelve seasons, while results flattered the underlying numbers by 16 points.
  • Results have often run ahead of the process, or behind it. Mourinho in 2017–18 finished 2nd with the 6th-best npxG difference. The 2024–25 side finished 15th, ten points below its expected total. Carrick's 2026 run took 2.29 points per game from about 1.7 expected.

Method, definitions and sources
Understat
Match-level xG, npxG, deep completions (passes completed within about 20 yards of goal) and expected points for all 456 league matches since August 2014. PPDA (opponent passes in their own 60% of the pitch, divided by our defensive actions there) per match up to April 2025, and per season for all twelve seasons, with league ranks from all 20 teams. Sources: public GitHub mirrors of Understat (MichaelKolby1/epl_results_prediction, AbrarHossainHimself/Understat_scraping_project, vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League, nabilo999/Scout-DB). The overlapping sources agree exactly on xG.
Managers
Matches are assigned to a manager by date. Caretaker spells: Carrick (2021, 2 league games), Rangnick (interim, 24), Van Nistelrooy (2), Fletcher (1). Carrick from 13 January 2026 counts as his own spell.
Event data, 2015–16
StatsBomb Open Data, all 380 Premier League matches. The definitions match the Pochettino portrait: possession sequences use StatsBomb possession chains; direct attacks start in the own half and reach a shot or box touch within 15 seconds; event-data PPDA counts tackles, interceptions and fouls; counter-presses are pressures within five seconds of losing the ball; zone dominance is the share of on-ball touches in 30 zones; xT is a 16×12 model trained on every pass and carry that season.
Recent seasons
Season totals of passes, long balls, possession won in the attacking third, tackles, interceptions, crosses and dribbles from SofaScore, combined with Understat xG and xG chain figures. Player figures are Understat season totals.
Top-six standard
For each season, the average of the six teams with the best npxG difference that year.

Caveats: Understat's PPDA and the event-data PPDA use different formulas, so compare each only with itself. Expected points are a model, not a verdict. The 2026–27 season is in progress and not included.

Data: Understat via public GitHub mirrors; StatsBomb Open Data (event data © StatsBomb, used under their open data terms); SofaScore season aggregates. Analysis and xT model by FourThreeThree.
