What public data can't see

Fullback underlaps and inverted fullbacks, rotations between the lines, rest-defence shape and the positions players take up are all positional and need tracking or event-location data (Opta, StatsBomb, SkillCorner). The data here only counts actions, so it describes what City do with the ball, not the structure that makes it possible. Goal-kick routines are visible only indirectly, through the goalkeeper's long-ball share.

          Each list is sorted by distance from the centre, so the purest examples of a style come first. Tempo blends two halves equally. Patience is passes per shot, adjusted for attacking output; set pieces drag it down, because corners produce shots with almost no passes. Retention is passes per match, pass accuracy, long-ball share and chipped-pass share, none of which set pieces affect. Pressing height is the share of ball recoveries made in the attacking third. Pressing intensity is tackles plus interceptions per match, divided by the opponent's share of possession (a stand-in for PPDA).

          How the index is built: metrics, weights and caveats

          Pep Index (v3, blended tempo). Weights in brackets; every metric is a z-score within its league and season.

          Tempo (×3)
          An equal blend of two halves. Patience: passes per shot, adjusted for attacking output, because the raw ratio also rises when a team simply creates little. Retention: passes per match, pass accuracy, long-ball share (negative) and chipped-pass share (negative). Set pieces drag patience down, since corners and free kicks produce shots with almost no passes; retention isn't affected by them.
          Poss % (×1.5)
          Share of possession.
          xGChain / xG (×1)
          Sum of every player's xGChain ÷ team xG: roughly how many players touch the ball in a move that ends in a shot. Stands in for possession-sequence length.
          Buildup share % (×1)
          xGBuildup ÷ xGChain: the share of attacking involvement that comes from build-up rather than the final pass or shot.
          GK long % (×−0.5)
          Goalkeepers' long balls as a share of their passes.
          High-regain % (×−0.5)
          Possession won in the attacking third as a share of all recoveries: the Red Bull/Klopp signature.
          Headed goals %
          Headed goals as a share of all goals. Not part of the index; a rough sign of how much a side scores from set pieces, shown so you can see where patience may be understated.
          v2 rank
          The previous version, which used passes per shot alone for tempo. Kept for comparison.
          Adjusted index
          What's left of the index after a linear fit on the log of minutes-weighted average player value (EA FC 26 valuations). It separates style from squad resources.

          Caveats:

          • Team stats belong to clubs, not coaches. Where a club changed coach mid-season, the row blends several coaches (see Share of season %). For example, Real Madrid 2025–26 is Alonso 53% and Arbeloa 47%.
          • Opta's direct speed, sequences per possession and true field tilt aren't public. The metrics here are the best public stand-ins.
          • Shot counts include set pieces, which public data can't separate out. Sides that take many shots from corners and free kicks look faster on passes per shot than their open play really is; blending in retention reduces this but can't remove it entirely.
          • League-relative scoring treats each league on its own terms. A 55% possession side in the Bundesliga isn't compared directly with one in La Liga.
