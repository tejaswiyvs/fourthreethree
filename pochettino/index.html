Mauricio Pochettino: a data portrait

How Pochettino's teams play, and how that changed over a decade: from the relentless pressing of his early Tottenham, through PSG's possession-heavy star team, a high-event Chelsea season and the US national team. Built from event data (every pass, press and touch), Understat's match-level xG and pressing numbers, and international results.

  • Peak Spurs pressed harder than anyone. In 2015–16 they allowed the fewest opponent passes per defensive action in the Premier League (lowest PPDA), took the most shots, and played the most short goal kicks.
  • The press faded steadily. Understat's PPDA rose every season from 2015–16 to his 2019 exit (6.4 → 11.0), while deep completions climbed: control replaced pressure.
  • PSG was a different team. Twice as many 10-pass moves as peak Spurs, slower attacks and 77% short goal kicks: possession first, press second.
  • Chelsea was his most open side. His highest npxG per game (1.89) and his worst npxG against (1.55).

Method, definitions and sources
Event data
StatsBomb Open Data: all 380 Premier League 2015–16 matches, and 26 PSG Ligue 1 matches from 2021–22. Pitch is 120×80 yards; each event is recorded in the acting team's attacking direction.
Possession sequences
StatsBomb possession chains. Completed passes per possession and 10+ pass possessions count only the team's own completed passes in its chain. Build-up attacks are 10+ pass possessions that reach a shot or a touch in the opposition box.
Direct attacks and speed
Direct attack: an open-play possession starting in the team's own half that reaches a shot or box touch within 15 seconds. Direct speed: median upfield metres per second across open-play possessions with three or more events.
PPDA
Event data: opponent open-play passes in their own 60% of the pitch, divided by the team's tackles, interceptions and fouls in that area. Understat uses a slightly different formula, so its values are lower; compare each source only with itself.
Pressures and counter-presses
StatsBomb pressure events; counter-presses are pressures within five seconds of losing the ball. High turnovers: possessions won within 40 metres of the opponent's goal.
Field tilt and zone dominance
Field tilt: a team's share of the final-third passes in a match. Zone dominance: share of all on-ball touches in each of 30 zones (6 lengthwise × 5 across), opponents flipped into the same direction.
Expected threat (xT)
A 16×12 grid model trained on every Premier League 2015–16 pass and carry (Karun Singh's method): the value of each zone is the chance it leads to a goal within the next few actions. A move's xT is the value at its end minus the value at its start; only positive gains are summed.
Understat
Match-level xG, npxG, PPDA, deep completions (passes completed within 20 yards of goal) and expected points, via public GitHub mirrors of Understat data. Tottenham matches up to his dismissal on 19 November 2019.
USMNT
Results from martj42/international_results. Elo uses a standard World Football Elo-style formula (home advantage, goal-difference multiplier, weights by competition) computed by FourThreeThree, so values won't match other Elo sites exactly. World Cup 2026 xG from ESPN.

Caveats: the PSG sample is 26 hand-picked matches rather than a full season; Spurs 2015–16 is a single season; and no public source tracks positional structure such as rotations or rest defence.

Data: StatsBomb Open Data (event data © StatsBomb, used under their open data terms); Understat match data via public GitHub mirrors; martj42/international_results; ESPN for World Cup 2026 xG. Analysis, xT model and Elo by FourThreeThree.
